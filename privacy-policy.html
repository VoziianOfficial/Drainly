1. Overview

Drainly is a free matching platform created to help homeowners connect with independent local service providers for gutter-related requests. The platform may collect information that users voluntarily submit when they request provider options, contact the site, or interact with the website.

Drainly does not perform gutter installation, replacement, repair, cleaning, or inspection services directly. Any work, pricing, quote, appointment, contract, or project communication is handled by independent providers.

2. Information we may collect

When you submit a request or contact form, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, service category, project notes, and other details you choose to provide.

We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device type, referring pages, pages visited, approximate location based on IP address, and site interaction data. This information may be used to maintain website functionality, improve the request experience, and protect the site from misuse.

3. How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to process a homeowner request, communicate with the user, help identify relevant independent provider options, improve website functionality, respond to inquiries, and comply with legal or operational obligations.

By submitting a request, you understand that information may be shared with independent providers or service partners for the purpose of responding to your gutter-related request.

4. Sharing with independent providers

Drainly may share submitted request details with independent local service providers or related partners who may contact you about the requested service category. These providers are independent businesses and are not employees, agents, or direct representatives of Drainly.

Homeowners are responsible for reviewing any provider before hiring, including verifying license, insurance, quote details, company information, service scope, and contract terms.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential site functionality, remember consent preferences, analyze basic site usage, and improve the user experience. More information is available on the Cookie Policy page.

6. Data security

Drainly uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect submitted information. However, no website, database, or online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User choices

You may choose not to submit a request through the website. You may also contact Drainly using the email listed on the site to ask questions about information you submitted through the platform.

Contact email: hello@drainlymatch.com

8. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for homeowners and adults seeking provider comparison options. It is not intended for children, and Drainly does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Updates to this policy

Drainly may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes may be posted on this page with an updated date.
