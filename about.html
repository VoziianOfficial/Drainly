Company story

Built around the moment before a homeowner chooses who to contact.

Many gutter projects begin with uncertainty. A homeowner may know that water is overflowing, gutters are sagging, or an older system needs attention — but may not know which type of provider to contact first.

Drainly focuses on that first step. The platform helps homeowners describe the request, understand the service category, and continue with independent providers that may fit the project type and location.

Drainly is not a direct gutter company, does not employ gutter technicians, and does not perform installation, replacement, repair, or cleaning work. Provider selection, verification, pricing, and project decisions remain between the homeowner and the independent company they choose.

Platform role

Drainly organizes the request. Independent providers handle their own services.

This keeps the experience clearer: homeowners start with one simple flow, then compare local companies before deciding who to contact or hire.
